About me

I'm a researcher interested in how AI systems are designed and governed, and how people come to trust them. My work brings together psychology, data science, and AI ethics, with a focus on health and assistive technology.

I graduated magna cum laude from Washington State University with a B.A. in Social Science, with training in psychology, statistics, and programming. In 2026 I completed graduate coursework in mathematics for AI and studied AI ethics and governance in the AI & Society master's program at PSL Research University in Paris. I'm now based in Austin, Texas.

My current interests:

  • Trust and decision-making when people use AI tools, especially in health and assistive technology
  • Machine learning for healthcare, aging, and agriculture
  • Responsible design and governance of AI systems, including the sustainable limits of AI infrastructure
