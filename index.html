Bio

I'm Roslyn — a data scientist and researcher passionate about the human side of AI. I graduated magna cum laude from Washington State University (B.A., Social Science) with training spanning social psychology, neuroscience, programming, and advanced statistics. I'm now based in Paris, pursuing an MSc in AI and Society at PSL Research University.

My work explores how AI systems are built, evaluated, and deployed responsibly:

  • Evaluating sustainable limits of AI infrastructure across regions
  • Using machine learning in healthcare and agriculture
  • Studying how AI shapes human decision-making in high-stakes contexts
